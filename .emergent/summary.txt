<product_requirements>
TerciLog is a student and trainer management application designed to handle session planning, attendance, billing, and student documentation.

**Core Features:**
*   **Teacher Dashboard:** Provides a Planning calendar view for creating, editing, and managing student sessions, and a Facturation (Billing) view for generating monthly financial reports with PDF/CSV export.
*   **Student Dashboard:** Allows students to view their schedule, confirm session attendance, provide feedback, and access documents.
*   **Parcours Élève (Student Record):** A comprehensive modal for teachers to manage a student's documents, categorized into Tests et évaluation and Supports de formation. Key functionality includes multi-format file uploads and the generation of a consolidated PDF summary that visually embeds the uploaded documents.
*   **Session Management:** Teachers can create single or multiple sessions for one or more students at once. The system automatically sends confirmation emails to students. Session status (e.g., confirmed by student) is tracked and reflected in the UI.
*   **Questionnaire Module:** Students can fill out a detailed Mes besoins en formation (Training Needs) questionnaire. The submitted form is then visible to the teacher in the student's record.

**Recent Implementation:**
The latest work involved building the Mes besoins en formation questionnaire, including the frontend form and backend endpoint for submission. The system was being updated to display these submitted questionnaires in a new Documents bénéficiaires tab within the teacher's Parcours Élève modal.
</product_requirements>
