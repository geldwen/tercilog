<product_requirements>
The TerciLog application requires a robust student progress tracking and quality analysis module.

1.  **Three-Part Questionnaire System**: Students must have access to three distinct questionnaires: besoin en formation, à mi-parcours, and fin de formation. Teachers must be able to view all submitted questionnaires for their assigned students, with options to consult, download a PDF, and email the results. When consulting, student answers must have a dark pink background.

2.  **Générer le Bilan Élève Feature**: On the teacher's view of a student's documents, a button should allow the generation of an AI-powered summary PDF. This PDF synthesizes data from the three completed questionnaires, calculates a progression score, and provides a textual summary. The button should be visible but disabled if the three questionnaires are not complete.

3.  **Bilan Qualité Dashboard**: A new page accessible to teachers that provides an aggregated view of student quality metrics.
    *   **Filtering**: Users must be able to filter the data by period (month/year) and by Parcours (e.g., Anglais, Bureautique).
    *   **KPIs**: The dashboard must display key performance indicators like the number of evaluated students, average progression, average satisfaction, and a visual bar for overall sentiment.
    *   **Student Table**: A detailed table listing all students matching the filters, showing the submission status (Green/Red dots) for each of the three questionnaires, and a button to remove a student from the report.
    *   **Data Logic**: The KPIs and table data must update dynamically when filters are changed.
</product_requirements>
